# Атлас: источник и запрос

Инструмент: встроенный ImageGen, transparent background. Платный внешний API не использовался; идентификатор внутренней модели и seed инструментом не раскрыты. Входы: `approved-storyboard.png` (приоритет стиля) и `pose-01-start.png` (детализация/идентичность), commit `36b689ebab402f65f60a60b1f15240cb60b4feaa` из PR #1121.

Исходный результат: RGBA PNG 1536×1024, SHA-256 `141a46c1a4602bf141856f3c2d86844a90f6e1cac32b3dd0698fb37fbb91df5e`. Оригинал сохранён локально в пакете. Для воспроизводимого компактного renderer source он перекодирован Pillow в WebP quality=100, method=4, exact=True. Это lossy WebP, не byte-exact копия PNG. Его SHA закреплён в `rig.json`; финальное видео отрендерено из этого WebP. Оригинальные восемь PNG такая перекодировка не затрагивает.

Запрос генерации:

> Use case: identity-preserve. Create a SINGLE rig-ready anatomical cutout SPRITE ATLAS for the EXACT SAME adult male exercise character in reference images. Reference 1 is owner-approved graphite black/white anatomical illustration with red active muscle accents, short black hair, dark shorts, white background. Reference 2 is high resolution identity and texture source. Preserve his face, body proportions, graphite muscle fibre shading, red abdominal/oblique/deltoid muscles, dark shorts. This is a production animation source, not a new design. Output a transparent 1536x1024 landscape atlas, NO labels, NO text, NO backdrop, NO checkerboard. Exactly FIVE separated disconnected pieces arranged left to right in five generous columns, all at the SAME anatomical scale, no pieces touching. 1: COMPLETE side-profile HEAD+NECK+TORSO+PELVIS+SHORTS as ONE continuous cutout, facing right, slightly leaning forward, NO arms and NO legs; reconstruct complete natural chest/abdomen/shoulder silhouette behind where arms would be. 2: ONE near-side anatomical UPPER ARM from round shoulder cap to elbow, vertical with shoulder at top; detailed graphite muscles, muted red deltoid, rounded overlap ends. 3: ONE FOREARM+HAND as one cutout, vertical elbow at top, wrist and gripping fist at bottom, fist holding a short black cylindrical ab-wheel handle pointing out of screen; NO WHEEL, detailed anatomically correct fingers and graphite fibres, rounded upper overlap end. 4: ONE complete THIGH from hip to knee, vertical hip at top, knee at bottom, anatomical white/graphite quadriceps, upper portion covered by the same dark shorts with broad overlap. 5: ONE bent SHIN+BARE FOOT as a continuous side-profile cutout, knee at upper-right, ankle/foot extending toward lower-left, matching the kneeling reference; detailed graphite musculature and natural toes. Exactly these five anatomical pieces, no complete person, no duplicated limbs, no wheels. Render all surfaces with the detailed premium anatomical graphite/red illustration of reference 1 and identity from reference 2. No flat vectors, no stick figures, no geometric mannequin parts. Every body surface must be fully illustrated to its edge, no cutaway open flesh. Alpha transparent background.

Результат не полностью выполнил запрос: есть лишний плечевой объём в torso и различия масштаба частей. Лицо и нижние ноги атласа не использованы; для них взят один оригинальный `pose-01-start.png`. Новый cutout остаётся `NEEDS_WORK`. Повтор генерации по этому запросу не гарантирует тот же atlas; повтор renderer использует сохранённый SHA-pinned source.
